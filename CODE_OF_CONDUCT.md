# Code of conduct

Threadbox contributors are expected to keep project spaces welcoming, constructive, and safe.

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or expose private information about another person.
- Keep reports factual and give others a reasonable opportunity to correct mistakes.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers will review reports confidentially and respond in proportion to the situation.
