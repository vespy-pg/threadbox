# Product naming

Status: ongoing exploration

Last updated: 2026-09-14

## Current position

`Threadbox` remains the working product and code name until a stronger replacement is selected. It
is unlikely to be the final name because `threadbox.com` is unavailable at an acceptable price,
`threadbox.app` is active, and an earlier collaboration product used the same name in a closely
related category.

There is currently no leading candidate.

## Naming criteria

The name should:

- Be short, memorable, and brandable.
- Be easy to pronounce and spell in both Polish and English.
- Work internationally without relying on English sounds such as `th`.
- Avoid doubled letters and other spelling patterns, such as `ss`, that make pronunciation or
  transcription less obvious across languages.
- Remain broad enough for an extensible work assistant, rather than describing only tasks, meetings,
  AI, or a single integration.
- Be allowed to use an invented word or a word unrelated to the product category.
- Prefer an exact, unregistered `.com` domain.
- Avoid ending in `k`.
- Generally avoid the letter `v`, unless the complete name is exceptionally strong.
- Avoid overusing pseudo-Latin or Romance-language patterns and names that always end in a vowel.
- Avoid phonetic similarities to vulgar, awkward, or misleading words in Polish and other important
  languages.

The `fox` ending is appealing because it can suggest a clever, agile, and friendly assistant, but it
is optional and should not constrain the search.

The `pilot` ending is also appealing. It suggests guidance and assistance while leaving the user in
control, and it can support a more professional brand than `fox`.

## Current candidate status

### AsistFox

- Status: weak possibility, described as "so-so" rather than a finalist.
- The exact `asistfox.com` domain appeared available when checked.
- It points in an interesting assistant-oriented direction and uses the liked `fox` ending.
- Its main weakness is spelling friction: English users naturally expect `AssistFox`, while the
  single-`s` form may look like a typo.
- `assistfox.com` is already registered, and `Assistfox` has also been used by a former Brazilian
  computer support company.

The next search may explore the broader idea of assistance or helpfulness, but it should avoid
forced misspellings and overly literal names.

### MeldFox

- Status: plausible candidate with significant brand-clearance risk, not rejected as a direct
  product duplicate.
- Its meaning fits the product well: it suggests bringing meetings, notes, decisions, people, and
  actions together.
- The exact `meldfox.com` domain appeared unregistered when checked.
- The reviewed public pages for products named Meld do not advertise meeting recording,
  transcription, meeting notes, or turning a recorded meeting into actions.
- `usemeld.ai` is nevertheless adjacent to the later part of the product vision: it connects email,
  Slack, calendars, documents, and project tools, then creates, routes, suggests, and completes work.
- The name needs a formal trademark and market-confusion review before it can become a finalist.

### TendoPilot

- Status: plausible candidate with significant brand-clearance risk, not rejected as a direct
  product duplicate.
- The exact `tendopilot.com` domain appeared unregistered when checked.
- Tendo AI is a builder for telephone receptionists. It does not advertise meeting capture,
  transcription, meeting notes, or post-meeting action extraction.
- It overlaps with planned secondary Threadbox capabilities through automated calls, appointment
  booking, and calendar integrations, rather than with the meeting-first core.
- The Tendo root is already used by several software products, so a formal trademark and
  market-confusion review is required before the name can become a finalist.

## Rejected names and directions

- `Zelpin` - rejected. It is also close to Zeplin and is used as a medicine name.
- `Cohervo` - rejected because its Polish pronunciation is too close to the vulgar word "kurwo".
- `Kanso` - rejected because it sounds too similar to "cancel".
- `Junto` - rejected because it evokes "junta" or "hunta".
- `Daro` - rejected because it sounds like a rural or gangster-style nickname for Dariusz in Polish.
- `PactoFox` - rejected because it strongly evokes the Polish hip-hop group Paktofonika.
- `PactoPilot` - rejected after collision screening. It is not a meeting-first competitor, but the
  active `PactPilot` product differs by only one letter and is also sold as an AI work assistant. It
  is available through web, mobile, Chrome, Slack, Telegram, and WhatsApp, which creates a much
  stronger naming-confusion risk than an ordinary adjacent product.
- Names ending in `k` - reject by rule.
- Names containing `plum` - reject as a direction.
- Names built mainly around `v` - strongly discouraged.

## Search process

1. Generate candidates from diverse sound patterns and concepts rather than alphabetically or from
   one repeated template.
2. Remove names with spelling problems, negative phonetic associations, or obvious category
   limitations.
3. Verify exact `.com` availability using registry data.
4. Screen the web for existing companies, products, applications, and close competitors.
5. Present candidates only after finding a batch of 15 worthwhile names with apparently available
   exact `.com` domains.
6. Use the user's reactions to refine the sound and personality of the next batch.
7. Before selecting or buying a domain, perform deeper trademark, company-name, app-store,
   domain-history, and social-handle checks for the finalists.

Domain availability is temporary and must be checked again immediately before registration. No
domain purchase has been authorized.
